Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective October 20, 2026. KubeAudit AI is operated by Sai Harsha Vardhan Mylavarapu (“we”, “us”). Questions: msharsha565@gmail.com

Short version: cancel anytime; your first payment comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee; after that we don't refund partial periods, but you keep Pro until your paid period ends.

How to cancel

Sign in, open your account (top right), and click Manage billing → Cancel plan. You can also email msharsha565@gmail.com from your account's email address and we'll cancel for you.

After you cancel, you won't be charged again. Pro stays on until the end of the monthly or yearly period you already paid for, then your account moves to the free plan. Your saved scans stay in your account; to keep using them after Pro ends, export what you need before then or upgrade again.

14-day money-back guarantee

If Pro isn't right for you, email msharsha565@gmail.com within 14 days of your first Pro payment (monthly or yearly) from your account's email address, and we'll refund that payment in full. Pro ends when the refund is issued.

Yearly renewals

We email a reminder before a yearly plan renews. If it renews and you didn't mean to keep it, ask within 14 days of the renewal charge for a full refund of that renewal.

Other cases

  • Outside those windows we don't refund partial months or years, but cancelling stops all future charges.
  • If you were charged by mistake (for example twice), or the Service was unavailable for a long time, contact us and we'll make it right.
  • If we discontinue Pro, we refund the unused part of any prepaid period.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. Banks usually show them within 5–10 business days.

Before disputing a charge

Please email us first; we answer billing questions within 2 business days and can usually resolve them faster than a bank dispute.
