Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026. KubeAudit AI is operated by Sai Harsha Vardhan Mylavarapu (“we”, “us”). Questions: msharsha565@gmail.com

This policy explains what KubeAudit AI collects, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

Scanning in your browser

When you paste, drop or upload YAML in the web app, it is scanned on your device. It is not sent to us unless you click Save. The app keeps your last files and your sign-in token in your browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off; clearing your browser data removes them.

What we store when you have an account

  • Account details: email address, name and profile picture link, and the account ID from GitHub, Google or Microsoft if you sign in with them.
  • Sign-in records: session tokens, email sign-in links and your API key, stored only as one-way hashes. For email sign-in we also keep the requesting IP address for an hour-long rate limit.
  • API usage: the time of each API scan and the number of resources and issues found, to count your monthly limit. The YAML you send to the API is scanned and not stored.
  • Saved scans (Pro): the name, score and findings, and the YAML files if you choose to save them; plus share links and your rule settings.
  • Billing (Pro): your plan, billing period and Stripe customer and subscription IDs. Card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store them.

Services that process data for us

  • Railway hosts the API and its database (United States).
  • GitHub hosts the web app (GitHub Pages) and provides GitHub sign-in.
  • Google and Microsoft provide sign-in when you choose them.
  • Resend sends sign-in emails.
  • Stripe processes payments and keeps billing records.
  • jsDelivr and Google Fonts deliver code and fonts the web app loads, so they receive your IP address when you open it.

Each of these handles data under its own privacy policy. We share only what they need to do their job.

Cookies

We set one short-lived cookie during GitHub, Google or Microsoft sign-in to protect against forged sign-in requests. It is deleted when sign-in finishes. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Deleting it (account page → Delete account) removes your account, saved scans, share links, sessions and API key from our database. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires. Hosting providers may keep server logs for a limited time.

Your choices and rights

You can see and change most of your data in the app, delete saved scans and share links at any time, and delete your account. To request a copy of your data or ask a question, email msharsha565@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, such as access, correction, deletion and objection; email us to use them.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, tokens and API keys are stored hashed, and shared reports never include your YAML. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

Children

The Service is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post updates here and email account holders about material changes.
