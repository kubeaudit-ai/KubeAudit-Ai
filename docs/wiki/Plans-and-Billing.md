## Upgrade

1. On [kubeaudit-ai.github.io](https://kubeaudit-ai.github.io/), click **Sign in** and continue with GitHub or Google.
2. Open your account (top right) and choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
3. Pay on the secure Stripe checkout page. KubeAudit AI never sees your card number.

Pro turns on as soon as the payment goes through.

## Cancel

Account (top right) → **Manage billing** → **Cancel plan**. You won't be charged again, and Pro stays on until the end of the period you already paid for, then the account moves to Free.

**Manage billing** is also where you update your card and download invoices.

## Refunds

- **14-day money-back guarantee** on your first Pro payment, monthly or yearly. Email us from your account's email address within 14 days for a full refund.
- **Yearly renewals:** you get a reminder before renewal, and you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of a renewal charge.
- Outside those windows, partial periods aren't refunded, but cancelling stops all future charges.
- Charged by mistake? Contact us and we'll make it right.

Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and usually show up in 5–10 business days. Full details: **[Refunds & cancellation](https://kubeaudit-ai.github.io/refund.html)**.

## Your data

An account stores only what you choose to save (scans you save, your rule settings) plus what's needed to sign you in and bill you. Scanning itself never uploads your YAML. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time: Account → **Delete account**.
